What We Make

Type of Rolls Products

A full range of roller coverings, formulated and built to the shore hardness and dimensions each application demands.

Nitrile Rubber

Oil and abrasion-resistant covering suited to demanding industrial roller duty.

Neoprene Rubber

Weather and chemical-resistant rollers for consistent long-run performance.

Natural Rubber

High resilience and grip for general-purpose textile and processing rollers.

P.U. Rubber Rollers

Polyurethane coverings for high wear resistance and precision printing work.

E.P.D.M. Rollers

Excellent ozone, weather and heat resistance for outdoor and hot-process lines.

Silicon Rubber

High-temperature & electrically resistant — also supplied as sleeves for corona treatment and bag-making machines.

Ebonite Rubber

Hard-cured rubber rollers for acid-proof tanks and demanding chemical duty.

Rubber Sleeve Expander

Banana roll construction for even fabric spreading on textile lines.

Nylon Stitch Brush Roll

Brush construction rollers for cleaning and finishing applications.

S.S. Rollers

Stainless steel rollers for corrosion-resistant, high-precision duty.
